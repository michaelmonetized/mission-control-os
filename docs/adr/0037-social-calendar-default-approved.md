# Social calendar: full suite with default-approved look-ahead

## First-ship social scope

Social calendar includes:

- Schedule + publish via Connected Accounts  
- **Categories / recycle** (content reuse patterns)  
- **Approval calendar** for Client review  
- **Full auto** mode (agency-configured autonomous scheduling within policy)

Inspired by production patterns on my.hustlelaunch.com-class tools (AI + calendar + publish), not a thin Buffer clone.

## Look-ahead approval queue

- Agency configures **N weeks** of look-ahead (per Client/Location/Campaign as product allows).
- Posts enter the visible approval calendar for that horizon so Client Users can review **before** publish time.

## Flipped approval logic (default approved)

**All Social Posts are Approved by default**—whether human-written or generated.

Client Users (and Agency staff) may:

- **Disapprove** individual posts (blocks publish) with **per-post edit notes** for the agency  
- **Edit** media, copy, link, etc. themselves (edited post remains on calendar; treat as still approved unless they also disapprove)

Posts **publish on schedule unless disapproved** (or hard-failed connectivity). No “waiting for electrician to click Approve” gate.

### Why this invert

From agency experience: if posting requires active client approval, busy trade clients never review → nothing ships → growth stalls → client blames agency → churn. Default-approved + easy disapprove/edit preserves control without hostage-taking silence.

## Consequences

- Notify Client Users of upcoming look-ahead window (digest), not “please approve each post.”
- Audit trail: created, edited by, disapproved by, notes, published at.
- Full auto still respects disapprovals and Connected Account health.
- Legal/compliance: Agency/Client contracts should reflect auto-publish unless opted out per post.

# Three-level tenancy: Agency → Client → Location

Tenant hierarchy is **Agency → Client → Location**. Agency is the SaaS customer; Client is the business the agency serves; Location is a physical/market unit under that Client (multi-location local SEO).

Data isolation, portals, crawls, citations, and analytics cache keys follow this tree. Location is a full product boundary—not merely a field on Client—because multi-location local SEO is a primary use case.

**Considered:** flat Agency-only (too weak for portals/isolation); Agency → Client only with Location as a property (simpler, but under-serves multi-location brands). Chose three levels despite more surface area.

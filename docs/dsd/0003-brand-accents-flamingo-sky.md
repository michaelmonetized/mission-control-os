# DSD-0003: Brand accents — Flamingo + Sky (Hustle Launch / Mocha)

## Lead brand pair

Mission Control inherits the **Hustle Launch brand pair**, aligned to **Catppuccin Mocha** names:

| Token | Mocha role | Hex (Mocha) | Use |
|-------|------------|-------------|-----|
| **brand.flamingo** | flamingo | `#f2cdcd` | Primary brand warm; logo accents, key CTAs, selected glow option A |
| **brand.sky** | sky | `#89dceb` | Primary brand cool; focus rings, links, live/ops neon, selected glow option B |

Together they are the **signature dual neon**—warm flamingo + cool sky—not a single monochrome accent.

## Pairing rules

- **Gradient / dual-glow:** flamingo → sky on primary marks, hero CTAs, active Agent/live states when you want “brand fully on.”  
- **Single-accent moments:** prefer **sky** for focus/keyboard/ops chrome; **flamingo** for brand moments, marketing surfaces, and warm emphasis.  
- **Never** use both at full intensity on every control—sparse cockpit (DSD-0001).

## Semantics (still Mocha)

Status and system colors stay Catppuccin semantic, not brand-only:

| Role | Mocha |
|------|--------|
| success | green |
| warning | yellow / peach |
| danger | red |
| info | sapphire / blue |
| muted | overlay / surface tokens |

Brand flamingo/sky **do not** replace success/danger.

## Glass + neon application

- Neon borders: sky-dominant for focus; flamingo for brand/selected primary; dual gradient stroke for hero only.  
- Neumorph controls: soft sky or flamingo rim light matching state.  
- Media kit: logo lockups explore flamingo, sky, and flamingo+sky variants on Mocha crust/base.

## House continuity

Same pair as Hustle Launch so Mission Control sits in the same visual family without copying HL marketing layouts.
